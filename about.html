About Cloud Kiss Beauty ☁️

Our Story

Cloud Kiss Beauty was born from a simple dream: to create beauty products that feel as soft and gentle as a cloud — wrapped in every shade of pink. Started in 2023 by a group of friends who believed beauty should be soft, romantic, and kind to your skin. What began as handmade pink lip balms shared among friends has grown into a full collection of pink-inspired beauty essentials, loved by many across the Philippines.

Our Mission

To bring out the confidence and beauty in everyone through gentle, high-quality products — celebrating every shade of pink. We promise safe ingredients, cruelty-free care, and affordable luxury that makes you feel beautiful inside and out.

Our Vision

A world where every glow is natural, every product is kind, and everyone finds their perfect shade of lovely — because life is better with a little touch of pink. 🌸
